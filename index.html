Questions, answered

Tap a question to open the answer.

Is it really 100% free?

Yes. Creating and hosting your one-page website is a goodwill service — no charge, no hidden costs, ever. We don't accept payment or donations for this work.

What do I need to provide?

Just your business details in a short form: what you do, your services, opening hours, contact details, and how you'd like your page to look — style and colours. Your own text and photos are welcome too.

Can I ask for changes?

Of course. You see a preview first and approve it before it is final, and you can ask us for small updates afterwards, as goodwill.

Can I get my files, or take my page down?

Anytime. You own your content — email us and we'll send you a copy of your page's files, or take the page down, whenever you ask.
